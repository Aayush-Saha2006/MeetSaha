About Me

I was born amidst the vibrant, historic energy of Dum Dum, Kolkata, a place where culture and intellectual legacy breathe through every street. However, I was truly shaped by the serene landscapes and industrious spirit of Ranchi, Jharkhand, where I was raised. This unique blend of backgrounds—the soul of Bengal and the grit of Jharkhand—has instilled in me a perspective that balances deep-rooted tradition with a forward-thinking drive for progress.

My journey is defined by a passion for transforming abstract ideas into tangible solutions. As a creator, I find immense fulfillment in the world of 3D modeling and additive manufacturing. I don't just design objects; I engineer daily-life enhancements, crafting functional 3D-printed tools that bridge the gap between digital precision and physical utility. Whether it is refining a mechanical component or creating a custom ergonomic solution, I believe that the future of design lies in its ability to empower the individual.

Beyond the physical realm, I am equally committed to the digital frontier. My work in web development is driven by a 'society-first' philosophy. I specialize in building platforms that are not just technically robust, but socially significant—tools designed to solve real-world problems and foster meaningful impact. From the intricate geometry of a 3D model to the complex architecture of a live website, my goal remains constant: to innovate with purpose, design with empathy, and contribute to a world where technology serves the greater good.
